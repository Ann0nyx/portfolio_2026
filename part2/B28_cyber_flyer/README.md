# B28) Produce a cyber safety flyer for (choose 1): elders, high school students, CEOs, Uni students.

In this activity, I designed and edited a cyber safety awareness flyer for elders using Canva. The flyer was created to educate elderly users about common online threats and provide simple cybersecurity advice in an easy to understand format. I used large headings, visual icons, and simple language so the information would be accessible to non-technical users and older audiences.


The flyer included information about phishing scams, suspicious links, password security, software updates and dangers of public Wi-Fi networks. I also explained the importance of using strong passwords, keeping personal information private, enabling two-factor authentication and regularly updating devices and applications to improve security. Additional safety advice and support contact information were included to encourage elders to seek help if they are unsure about suspicious online activity.


Overall, this activity helped improve my ability to communicate cybersecurity concepts visually and clearly while designing educational material targeted toward vulnerable users who are commonly targeted by online scams and phishing attacks.
