# B4)Participate in 3 in-class activities in labs

I participated in the Thursday lab sessions held from 10 am to 12 pm, where the facilitators conducted several interactive cybersecurity related activities. I attended the following:

### 1) Activity 1-Cryptography Activity

During one of the lab sessions, I participated in a cryptography-based activity where students were divided into teams and asked to create a hidden message using encryption techniques. My group created several sentences and encrypted them using a combination of cryptographic encryptions and our own creative encoding logic. After completing the encryption process, groups moved around the classroom attempting to decrypt and solve the encrypted messages created by other teams.
This activity helped me better understand the purpose of cryptography in protecting information and how different encryption methods can make messages difficult to interpret without the correct key or logic. It also demonstrated how creativity and complexity can strengthen security while highlighting the challenges involved in cryptanalysis and code-breaking.

### 2) Activity 2 - Access Control Activity

In another lab session, I participated in an access control activity whefe students were again divided into groups. Each group was required to design a set of access control rules that only members of their own group could satisfy. The rules had to work in a way that allowed every legitimate group member access while preventing students from other groups from meeting the requirements.

After creating the rules, groups tested each other's access control systems to determine whether unauthorised users could bypass the restrictions. This activity helped me understand how authentication and access control mechanisms are designed and the importance of creating secure but practical security policies, It also showed how weak or predictable rules can potentially allow unauthorised access.

### 3) Activity 3- Risk Management Activity

Another lab session conducted the risk management activity where, students worked in groups and were given a checklist of items and conditions to assess throughout the building. My group walked around different areas identifying potential risks, hazards and security concerns based on the provided criteria. We evaluated the likelihood and potential impact of different risks and discussed possible ways they could be reduced or managed.
This activity provided practical experience in identifying and assessing risks within a real environment. It helped me understand the importance of risk management in cybersecurity and physical security, particularly how organisations must continuously evaluate vulnerabilities and implement controls to reduce potential threats.
