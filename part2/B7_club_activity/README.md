# B7 - Participate in CS/DS/cybersecurity clubs' activity

## Description

I participated in an AI workshop organised by the UWA AI Club in collaboration with Visagio on 6 May 2026. The workshop focused on how Artificial Intelligence is transforming modern consulting workflows, including problem scoping, business analysis and delivering recommendations to clients.

## Activity Performed

During the workshop, participants were given a real world marketing case study involving a struggling travel agency business. Working in a team of three, we were required to analyse the company’s problems and propose innovative solutions that could improve business performance and profitability.

Our team discussed several approaches involving AI technologies, including AI-powered customer recommendation systems, automated marketing analysis, chatbot-based customer support, predictive analytics for travel trends and personalised travel package generation.

We also explored how AI could help reduce operational costs while improving customer engagement and decision-making efficiency.

## Skills and Knowledge Gained

This activity improved my understanding of practical AI applications in business environments, teamwork and collaborative problem solving, business analysis and solution design, how consulting firms apply AI to real client scenarios, presenting innovative technological solutions under time constraints and business analysis and solution design.

## Reflection

The workshop demonstrated how AI is increasingly integrated into consulting and business strategy. It provided valuable insight into how technical and analytical skills can be applied to solve real organisational problems. Participating in the activity also strengthened my communication and critical thinking skills while exposing me to industry-style consulting practices.
