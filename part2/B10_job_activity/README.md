# B10 - Participate in a cybersecurity activity as part of job/internship/volunteering etc.

As part of my work as a medical receptionist at Cannington Health Clinic, I participated in cybersecurity-related activities involving the secure handling of sensitive patient information, healthcare correspondence and digital medical systems. My responsibilities included handling confidential mail, patient appointments, referrals, billing systems, shredding documents and medical records while maintaining patient privacy and confidentiality. 


I also followed secure workplace practices such as keeping open paperwork face down on desks to prevent sensitive patient information from being accidentally viewed or leaked by other patients or visitors. Additionally, I became more aware of cybersecurity risks such as phishing emails, weak passwords, unauthorised access to patient data, and the importance of 


securely handling healthcare information within a professional environment. Through this experience, I learned how cybersecurity and privacy protection are extremely important in healthcare organisations due to the large amount of confidential personal and medical information managed daily.
