# B14 - Teach your friends about cybersecurity topic of your choice

## Explanation

In this activity, I taught my friends about several cybersecurity topics related to everyday online behaviour, including social engineering, phishing scams, fake public Wi-Fi networks, QR code scams, password change and online impersonation attacks. The discussions were conducted through WhatsApp conversations and messages where I explained how attackers often manipulate people emotionally instead of directly attacking computer systems.

Initially, my friends did not fully understand how serious these threats could be. Anshika mentioned they would likely trust messages that looked authentic, while Sai regularly used public Wi-Fi networks without considering the security risks involved and Sonna still reuse her passwords. This showed a lack of awareness regarding phishing techniques, fake Wi-Fi hotspots, password management and information theft.

During the conversations, I explained how attackers impersonate trusted organisations or IT support staff to trick users into revealing passwords or sensitive information. I also explained how fake Wi-Fi networks can be used in Man-in-the-Middle (MITM) attacks to monitor online activity and potentially steal data. Real-world examples and simple explanations were used to make the topics easier to understand and relatable.

I also provided practical safety advice, including avoiding suspicious links, verifying messages before trusting them, checking for HTTPS websites, avoiding sensitive logins on public Wi-Fi, and using mobile data or VPNs for important activities.I also explained to Sonna how attackers use leaked passwords from one breached website to gain access to other accounts when the same password is reused across multiple services.I also discussed safer security practices such as using password managers and enabling multi-factor authentication (MFA).

Overall, this activity demonstrated the importance of cybersecurity awareness for everyday users. It also helped improve my ability to communicate technical cybersecurity concepts clearly to non-technical individuals while encouraging safer online behaviour.
