# B9 - Participate in an industry-related cybersecurity event

## Description

I participated in the Coders for Causes Industry Night 2026.An industry networking and technology event held at the University of Western Australia. The event included representatives and professionals from major technology and consulting companies such as Atlassian, Commonwealth Bank, EY, Fortescue, IMC and Visagio.

## Activity Performed

During the event, professionals from different organisations introduced their companies and discussed the work they perform within the technology, cybersecurity, data science, consulting and software engineering industries.

The speakers shared insights into real-world projects, workplace culture, career pathways and the importance of technical and communication skills in industry environments. They also further discussed on their internship opportunities and skill set that an intern can gain through the workforce experience.

I also had the opportunity to network with several professionals, including representatives from Commonwealth Bank and Atlassian. Through these conversations, I learned more about cybersecurity roles, industry expectations, internship pathways and how large organisations manage technology and security operations.

## Reflection

The event was highly valuable because it allowed me to directly interact with industry professionals and gain insight into the practical side of cybersecurity and technology careers. Listening to experts from different organisations helped me better understand the variety of roles available within the industry and the skills employers look for in students and graduates. The networking experience also increased my confidence when communicating with professionals in technical fields.
