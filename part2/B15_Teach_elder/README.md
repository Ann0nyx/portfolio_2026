# B15 - Teach an elderly person about cybersecurity topic of your choice

## Topic taught: Website Security and Phishing Scams

In this activity, I taught my grandfather about basic cybersecurity concepts related to website safety, phishing scams, password security, and safe internet browsing practices. The discussion focused on helping him recognise suspicious online behaviour and avoid common scams targeting elderly users.

Initially, he had very little awareness regarding cybersecurity risks. He mentioned that he did not know most of his passwords and often clicked links without verifying whether they were legitimate. This demonstrated how elderly users can be more vulnerable to phishing attacks and online scams due to limited technical knowledge.

During the discussion, I explained how phishing scams work and how attackers create fake websites, emails or messages designed to trick users into revealing passwords or personal information. I advised him not to click random links, especially from unknown messages or emails and to always verify websites before entering sensitive information. I also explained the importance of strong passwords, regularly updating passwords, and using a password manager to safely store login credentials instead of reusing or forgetting passwords.

Overall, this activity highlighted the importance of cybersecurity awareness among elderly individuals, as they are often common targets for online scams and phishing attacks. It also helped improve my ability to explain technical cybersecurity concepts in simple and understandable language.
